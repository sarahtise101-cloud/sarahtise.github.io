Hello, I'm Sarah

I am a senior at UIC majoring in Mechanical Engineering with hands-on experience in biomedical engineering research, aerospace engineering design/fabrication, and mechanical/manufacturing engineering practices in the railroad industry as well as the heavy industrial equipment industry.

I’ve worked on a variety of practical projects which are all listed on this site. My first internship was at OPIRA Lab as a Machine Learning for Skin Melanoma Diagnosis Intern, conducting research in optical and photoacoustic imaging. Last summer, I completed a summer internship at TTX Company as a Maintenance & Modification Engineering Intern, where I supported mechanical evaluations, system improvements, and gained practical experience with SolidWorks, AutoCAD, Excel, and time studies. This past summer I completed an internship at EH Wachs, a division of ITW, as a manufacturing engineer intern, where I implemented new manufacturing processes, or optimized existing ones.

In addition, I serve as Vice President of UIC’s AIAA section, where I lead rocketry projects ranging from designing custom carbon fiber composite mid-power rockets to leading the vehicle and safety teams for our participation in the 2025-2026 NASA USLI Competition.

Feel free to browse this site to get a closer look at my projects and experiences!
